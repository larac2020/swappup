## 4. How a Sale Works: From Purchase to Payment Release

1. When a buyer purchases a listing, payment is held securely by our payment processor.
2. The seller completes the name change with the airline and confirms this in-app.
3. **The buyer's confirmation that the name change is correct is mandatory, but it does not by itself trigger payment release.** Payment to the seller is released **24 hours after the ticket's scheduled departure**, regardless of when the buyer's confirmation happened — unless the buyer reports an issue before that point.
4. There are two separate windows in which a buyer can report a problem:
   - **Shortly after the name change** — if the airline's confirmation shows the wrong name or details, the buyer can flag this immediately using the in-app reporting tool.
   - **Up until 24 hours after the flight** — for any issue relating to actual use of the ticket (e.g. the name change was not honoured at check-in).
5. **Disputes raised under this section are limited to issues with the ticket or the name on it.** They do not cover unrelated personal circumstances (e.g. a missed flight, illness, or other force majeure affecting the buyer), which are the buyer's own responsibility.
6. Once funds are released to the seller, Swappup cannot reverse the payment, and the buyer must pursue any further claim directly against the seller and/or the airline.
