# Airline Transferability Research (Hand-Verified)

Founder's hand-verified research table covering the 52 carriers currently tracked by Swappup.

**Documentation only.** This file is a research record — it does not drive any application logic and no database values were changed to produce it. The live values used by the app remain those in the airline fee registry surfaced on the public FAQ.

**Disclaimer:** Airline name-change policies and fees change without notice, and published policy pages are frequently incomplete or contradicted by call-centre practice. Fees below are indicative, per person and per flight unless stated otherwise, and must be re-confirmed before relying on them commercially.

Last reviewed: 22 August 2026.

## Status legend

- **Transferable** — full passenger name change permitted by published policy.
- **Partially transferable** — permitted only on some fare families or through restricted channels.
- **Not transferable** — name changes refused; at most minor spelling corrections.
- **Ambiguous** — published sources conflict or are silent; requires direct confirmation from the airline.
